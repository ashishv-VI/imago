**Q1: What makes Imago Photography different from other school photographers?**
Imago Photography specialises exclusively in school photography across the UK, which means every part of our process — equipment, editing, delivery — is built around the needs of schools and the children they photograph. We shoot in high resolution using professional-grade cameras and lighting, and every image is individually edited before delivery. Schools get consistently sharp, vibrant photos that hold up at any print size, from wallet photos to yearbook spreads.

**Q2: What image quality and resolution do Imago's school photos come in?**
Every photograph produced by Imago Photography is delivered in full high resolution — suitable for large-format printing, digital displays, and yearbook production without any loss of quality. We do not compress or reduce file sizes during delivery. Whether a parent is ordering a small print or a school is producing an A3 display, the image quality remains the same. High-resolution output is standard, not an upgrade.

**Q3: Can Imago Photography handle more than just individual portrait days?**
Yes. Imago Photography covers the full school photography calendar: individual portraits in the autumn term, class and year-group photos, sports team shoots, leavers' photography, and special school events throughout the year. Having a single trusted photographer across all shoots means consistent lighting, style, and quality in every image a school produces.

**Q4: Which areas of the UK does Imago Photography cover?**
Imago Photography works with schools across the United Kingdom, including schools that are more than 200 miles from our base. We are not a local-only service — we travel nationwide to deliver consistent, professional photography to schools regardless of their location.

**Q5: How do schools book a photography session with Imago?**
Schools can get in touch with Imago Photography directly through the enquiry form on our website or by calling our team. Once we have your details, we will discuss your requirements, confirm your preferred dates, and send across everything you need ahead of the shoot. We recommend booking early, especially for autumn term portrait days, as availability fills up quickly.

**Q6: How long does it take to receive photos after a school shoot?**
Turnaround times vary depending on the size and type of shoot. We will always confirm the expected delivery timeline at the point of booking. All images go through individual editing before delivery, so you can be confident that what arrives has been quality-checked — not just exported in bulk.

**Q7: How are school photos delivered to parents?**
Imago Photography uses a secure online system where parents can view, select, and order their child's photos at their own convenience. Schools receive access details to share with families. There is no obligation to purchase, and parents can order prints, digital downloads, or packages directly through the platform.

**Q8: Does Imago Photography comply with GDPR and safeguarding requirements for photographing children?**
Yes. Imago Photography follows strict GDPR and safeguarding guidelines for all school shoots. We work closely with schools to ensure appropriate consent is in place before any photography takes place, and all images are handled and stored securely. Our photographers are DBS checked and experienced in working within school environments.

**Q9: Can parents order additional prints or products after the original order?**
Yes. Parents can return to the online ordering platform after their initial order to purchase additional prints, products, or digital files. Images are retained for a set period after delivery, giving families time to order at their convenience.

**Q10: What happens if we are not happy with the photos from our shoot?**
Imago Photography's internal satisfaction rating from school clients is 4.93 out of 5. If for any reason a school or family has a concern about their photos, we ask them to contact us directly so we can make it right. Our reputation is built on the schools that come back to us year after year, and we take every piece of feedback seriously.
