**Q: What makes Imago Photography different from other school photographers?**

Imago Photography specialises exclusively in school photography across the UK, which means every part of our process — equipment, editing, delivery — is built around the needs of schools and the children they photograph. We shoot in high resolution using professional-grade cameras and lighting, and every image is individually edited before delivery. Schools get consistently sharp, vibrant photos that hold up at any print size, from wallet photos to yearbook spreads. It is the combination of specialist focus and uncompromised image quality that sets Imago apart.

---

**Q: What image quality and resolution do Imago's school photos come in?**

Every photograph produced by Imago Photography is delivered in full high resolution — suitable for large-format printing, digital displays, and yearbook production without any loss of quality. We do not apply compression or reduce file sizes during delivery. Whether a parent is ordering a small print or a school is producing an A3 display, the image quality remains the same. High-resolution output is a standard part of every Imago shoot, not an upgrade.

---

**Q: Can Imago Photography handle more than just individual portrait days?**

Yes. Imago Photography covers the full school photography calendar: individual portraits in the autumn term, class and year-group photos, sports team shoots, leavers' photography, and special school events throughout the year. Having a single trusted photographer across all shoots means consistent lighting, style, and quality in every image a school produces — which matters when photos appear side by side in a yearbook or on a school website.
