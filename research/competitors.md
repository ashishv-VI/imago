## Priority Actions Based on Competitor Gaps

1. **Own the quality angle** — no competitor leads with HD photography as strongly as Imago can. Every blog and page should reinforce this.
2. **Target decision-stage keywords** — "how to choose a school photographer UK" and "what to look for in a school photography company" are high-intent. We have blogs in progress for both.
3. **Build AI / LLM presence** — competitors are absent from Quora, Reddit, and Medium. Imago being active here is a structural advantage in AI search.
4. **Education directories** — once the website is technically clean, submit to UK education directories to build backlinks competitors already have.
