## Context

AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) were presented to the Imago team on the 3 June 2026 call by Ashish. This is a new workstream running alongside traditional SEO — focused on making Imago visible across LLMs including ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

The approach is ecosystem-based: the more Imago is mentioned and cited across authoritative third-party platforms, the more likely AI tools are to surface the brand in relevant responses. Every off-page activity (Quora, Reddit, guest blogs, third-party articles) contributes to both traditional SEO and LLM visibility.
